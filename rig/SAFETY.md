# Rig safety (lane: rig)

Upstream's arm rules come first: `upstream/AGENTS.md` and `upstream/README.md`. **The arms have no brakes.**

## Before every session
- [ ] Followers resting, supported, where they can go limp — that's where they park.
- [ ] Gripper angles and CAN adapters set in `upstream/.env` (no defaults: a missing value fails instead of swapping arms).
- [ ] One person on the leader arms or the `p` (park) key for the whole run. Nobody else in arm reach.

## Cold practice (default)
- Griddle **off and cold** for any new or changed prompt.
- Use a real tortilla, but you can swap cheese/filling for paper confetti until pours are reliable.

## Hot runs
- [ ] The same commit worked cold at least 3 times in a row.
- [ ] Griddle on its lowest useful setting; nothing flammable near it; cables routed away from the hot plate.
- [ ] Heat-proof gloves and a fire extinguisher within reach.
- [ ] Spatula has a heat-proof handle the gripper can hold; fingers stay off the griddle surface.
- If anything looks wrong: take over with the leaders, or `p`. Don't wait for voice.
