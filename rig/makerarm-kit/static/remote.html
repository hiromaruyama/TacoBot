Keyboard (hold keys, several at once): AD base · WS shoulder · IK elbow · JL wrist yaw · ↑↓ wrist flex · ←→ wrist roll · O/C open/close gripper · Z/X speed −/+ · hold Shift for precise · Space stop · Q/E switch arm. Drag a joystick (touch works too); let go and the arm stops. Moving needs control; Hold and Park work for everyone.
